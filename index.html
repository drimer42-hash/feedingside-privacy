Feeding Side

Privacy Policy & Support — effective August 31, 2026

The short version

Feeding Side collects no data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

When you tap “Left side” or “Right side,” the app saves the side and the time to private storage on your device (an App Group container shared only between the app and its widgets). This data never leaves your iPhone: there is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. The app makes no network connections and we receive no information about you or your use of the app.

Widgets, Siri & Shortcuts

The Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets read the same on-device data. Siri and Shortcuts actions are processed by Apple on your device under Apple’s privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Tap any entry in the History list (or any bar in the chart) to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers and collects no data from anyone.
