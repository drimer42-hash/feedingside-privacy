Feeding Side

Privacy Policy & Support — effective September 26, 2026

The short version

Feeding Side has no accounts, no names, no emails and no passwords. Your log lives on your phone. If — and only if — you pair with a partner, the feeding log is also stored on our server so both phones can show it. Nothing is used for advertising or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

What the app stores on your phone

When you tap “Left side” or “Right side,” or log a bottle, the app saves the side, the time and any amount in private storage on your device, shared only with its widgets. Photos you add for each side are stored the same way and never leave your phone.

Sharing with a partner

Sharing is optional and off until you turn it on. When you tap “Share with your partner,” the app creates an anonymous identity for that phone — a random ID with no name, email or phone number attached — and stores your feeding log (side, time, bottle amount) on our database, hosted by Supabase in the United States. The other phone joins by typing a one-time code that expires after 15 minutes.

Only phones that joined your log with a code can read or change it; database access rules keep every family’s log separate. We do not look at logs, and we never combine them with any other information. Photos are never uploaded.

What we collect

Only if you turn on sharing: the feeding log itself and the anonymous ID that lets your two phones find it. It is used solely to make the app work. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Widgets, Siri & Shortcuts

The Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets read the same on-device data. Siri and Shortcuts actions are processed by Apple on your device under Apple’s privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Tap any entry in the History list (or any bar in the chart) to delete it — the deletion travels to your partner’s phone too. To remove your data from our server, open “Synced with your partner” and tap “Stop sharing”: once no phone is left in a shared log, the log and every entry in it are permanently deleted. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that phone. You can also email us to have a shared log deleted.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers and collects no personal information from anyone.
